# Reset your password

**Who:** Users with a portal account.

## Request a reset

1. Open your portal's sign-in page.
2. Select the forgot-password link.
3. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit.
4. Check your inbox and spam folder for the reset email.
5. Open the complete reset link and enter and confirm your new password.
6. Follow the on-screen confirmation to return to the portal.

Use the newest reset email if you have made more than one request. Do not share reset links with other people.

## If you do not receive an email

Check that you used the address your organization invited. A reset confirmation does not establish that an account exists for every entered address. Ask the portal staff to check your user record if you are unsure.

Staff can use the accepted user's **Copy Password Reset Link** action in **Users** to provide a reset link directly to that person.

## If the link fails

Request a new reset if the previous link is expired or already used. Open the full URL from the message, including its token fragment. If you are setting up your account for the first time, follow [Accept an invitation](../for-clients/accept-invitation.md) instead.

## Passkey sign-in

If a registered passkey is available, you can use it to sign in without entering your password. Manage those credentials from **Security** after signing in. See [Use passkeys](./passkeys.md).
