# Accept an invitation

**Who:** People invited to create a ClientShare account.

Your invitation connects your email address to a portal. The organization that invited you controls which client workspaces your account can access.

## Set up your account

1. Open the invitation email from the organization you work with.
2. Follow its invitation link to **Set your password**.
3. Enter a **New password** and the same value under **Confirm password**.
4. Select **Set password**.

After the password is saved, the portal signs you in. You may be offered the option to add a [passkey](../security/passkeys.md) for future sign-ins.

Keep the portal address so you can return later. You do not need to reuse the invitation link for each visit.

## Find your documents

Open **My Files** and the client workspace or folder you need. Your account can be assigned to more than one client. Follow [Send and receive documents](./send-and-receive-documents.md) for uploads and downloads.

## If the invitation does not work

- **Passwords do not match:** re-enter both fields with the same password.
- **Invalid or missing invite token:** reopen the complete original link. Do not remove the part after `#`.
- **Expired or already-used invitation:** ask the sender to resend it, or sign in normally if you have already accepted.
- **No email:** check spam and ask the sender to verify your address and resend the invitation.
- **Missing workspace:** ask the sender to check your client assignments.

If you received a secure link rather than an account invitation, use [Open a secure link](./open-secure-link.md) instead.
