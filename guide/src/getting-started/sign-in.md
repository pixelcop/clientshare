# Sign in and find your way around

**Who:** Staff and invited client users. Secure-link recipients should follow [Open a secure link](../for-clients/open-secure-link.md).

## Sign in

1. Open the portal address provided by your organization.
2. Enter your email address and password on the sign-in page.
3. Submit the form. If you have registered a passkey, you can use the passkey sign-in option instead.

New users should first follow their [invitation email](../for-clients/accept-invitation.md). If you have forgotten your password, use the password-reset link on the sign-in screen.

## Staff navigation

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Clients** | Find client workspaces and open their files or user list. |
| **Activity** | Review unread and read activity notifications. |
| **Users** | Invite people and manage their roles and client assignments. |
| **Settings** | Administrators customize branding and portal settings. |
| **User Guide** | Open this documentation. |

Use the dropdown next to a row's main action to see additional actions. On narrow screens, buttons may display icons rather than full labels.

## Client navigation

Client users work from **My Files** and can view **Activity**. The files available to you depend on your assigned clients. A secure-link session shows the access type and expiry set by the sender.

Open the user menu to access **Security** for your passkeys or to sign out. Sign out when you finish using a shared device.

## If your screen looks different

Your role controls which menus appear. Branding can change the title, colors, and logo. If a client or document is missing, ask the portal administrator to check your assignment rather than creating a second account.

See [Troubleshooting](../help/troubleshooting.md) for sign-in and access problems.
