# Sign in and find your way around

**Who:** Staff and invited client users. Secure-link recipients should follow [Open a secure link](../for-clients/open-secure-link.md).

## Sign in

1. Open the portal address provided by your organization.
2. Enter your **Email** and select **Continue**.
3. Follow the passkey prompt if offered, or enter your **Password** and select
   **Sign In**. If passkey sign-in fails or is canceled, the portal can fall back
   to password sign-in when your account has a password.

New users should first follow their [invitation email](../for-clients/accept-invitation.md). If you have forgotten your password, use the password-reset link on the sign-in screen.

## Staff navigation

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Clients** | Find client workspaces and open their files or user list. |
| **Activity** | Review unread and read activity notifications. |
| **Users** | Invite people and manage their roles and client assignments. |
| **Shared with me** | Open files and folders shared directly with your account. |
| **Settings** | Administrators customize branding, portal settings and access roles. |
| **User Guide** | Open this documentation. |

Use the dropdown next to a row's main action to see additional actions. On narrow screens, buttons may display icons rather than full labels.

## Client navigation

Client users work from **My Files** and can view **Activity**. Use **Client workspace**
above the file list to switch between assigned workspaces. Individual file and
folder shares appear in **Shared with me**; accounts without a whole workspace
may land there instead. A secure-link session shows the access type and expiry set by the sender.

Open the user menu to access **Security** for your passkeys or to sign out. Sign out when you finish using a shared device.

## If your screen looks different

Your organization role and scoped document permissions control which menus
appear. Internal Members do not automatically have client or user management.
Administrators can start or resume **Collect a document** from **Help**.
Branding can change the title, colors, and logo. If a client or document is
missing, ask the portal administrator to check your assignment rather than
creating a second account.

See [Troubleshooting](../help/troubleshooting.md) for sign-in and access problems.
