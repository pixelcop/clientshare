# Welcome to ClientShare

ClientShare gives your team and your clients one place to exchange documents. Staff organize client workspaces, upload files, invite users, and send time-limited secure links. Clients open their portal or a link to send and receive documents.

## Choose your starting point

- **Setting up or managing a portal?** Follow [Your first client exchange](./first-client-exchange.md).
- **Received an invitation?** Start with [Accept an invitation](../for-clients/accept-invitation.md).
- **Received a document link?** Read [Open a secure link](../for-clients/open-secure-link.md).
- **Already signed in?** Learn to [send and receive documents](../for-clients/send-and-receive-documents.md).

## How the portal is organized

A **client** is a workspace for an organization or person you work with. It contains a root folder, subfolders, and files. A **user** is someone who signs in. A client user can be assigned to one or more client workspaces; creating a client does not itself create a user account.

A **secure link** provides access to a client's files without requiring the recipient to create an account. The administrator chooses the link's permissions and expiry. Links are client-wide, so consider everything in that workspace when sharing.

See [Roles and access](./roles-and-access.md) before inviting staff or sharing files.

## Using this guide

Use the sidebar to browse tasks or **Search** to find a screen label or problem. On smaller screens, open the navigation menu to browse sections. The **User Guide** link in the app opens this documentation.

Screens and available actions depend on your role and the version your portal runs. Hosted and self-hosted portals use the same core document workflows. Your portal may show your organization's logo, title, and colors.

For installation and server configuration, see the [self-hosting README](https://github.com/pixelcop/clientshare#quick-start).
