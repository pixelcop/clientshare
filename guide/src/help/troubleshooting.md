# Troubleshooting

Start with the section matching what you see. If you need help from the organization running your portal, include the page, client and folder names, approximate time, and exact error message. Share a screenshot with access links and unrelated personal details cropped out.

## I cannot sign in

1. Check that you opened the correct organization's portal.
2. Use the email address on your invitation.
3. If your invitation is still pending, [accept it](../for-clients/accept-invitation.md) first.
4. If you forgot your password, [request a reset](../security/reset-password.md).
5. For passkey problems, try another registered credential or password sign-in.

Ask portal staff to verify your account if the problem persists. Do not create another account just to work around a missing assignment.

## My secure link no longer works

Open the complete original link. If it has expired or been revoked, ask the sender for a replacement. Only the sender's administrator can provide new permissions or a later expiry.

If you opened a secure link while using a staff account, sign in again to return to the account's normal navigation.

## A client, folder, or action is missing

- Check the current client and breadcrumb.
- Ask staff to verify your client assignments.
- Confirm whether you are signed in as a user or using a link session.
- Check the link's access label: Read-only cannot upload, and Upload-only cannot download.
- Ask whether the item has been deleted.

Settings and secure-link management are administrator features. Client users do not have staff deletion controls.

## My upload failed

Read the upload error and check which files are already in the list. Retry only missing files. Try a smaller batch and keep the page open until completion.

For duplicate names, use the rename prompt to choose distinct filenames. For unsupported folder dragging, create folders in the portal and upload files separately. Storage and file-size errors may require help from the portal operator.

## A document does not preview or download

Use the row's download action if the preview type is unsupported. Check your browser's download list. With an upload-only link, ask the sender for read access rather than repeatedly retrying.

If a supported file fails, ask the sender to check that the upload completed and the original document opens correctly.

## I did not receive an email

Check spam and verify the recipient address. Upload notifications may be queued. Staff can inspect [pending email](../activity/email-notifications.md), resend a pending invitation, or provide the intended recipient a fresh access link.

An empty queue does not prove delivery to an inbox. Persistent delivery failures need investigation by the portal operator.

## I uploaded the wrong file

Tell the sender which client, folder, and filename are affected. Staff can delete the incorrect item. If uploading a correction, use a clearly different filename and explain which version replaces the old one.

## I accidentally deleted something

There is no user-facing undo or trash recovery screen. Contact the portal operator promptly with the exact item and deletion time. Recovery depends on their storage and backups; it is not guaranteed by the app.
