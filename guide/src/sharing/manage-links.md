# Review and revoke secure links

**Who:** People with permission to manage sharing for the client, folder or file.

## Find a client's links

Open **Manage client access → Links** for the whole client, or a file or folder's
**Share → Links** section. Staff can also open **Links** from the client directory.

Choose **Use link** or **Details & email** to copy or email an existing link.
Check its permissions and expiry before sending it.

Select **Show history** in the sharing dialog to see past invitations and links.
In the client's link list, use **Expired & revoked history** to see links that no longer work.

**Details & email → Link history** shows when a link was created and last used,
if recorded. It does not tell you who read or approved the documents.

## Revoke a link

1. Identify the link by its creation time, permissions and expiry.
2. Select **Revoke**.
3. Confirm that you want to stop the link working.
4. Check the link history to confirm it is revoked.

Revoking a link blocks further access through it, including uploads and downloads
from an already-open tab. Other links and account access are unchanged. Emails
for that link that have not been sent are canceled. Already-sent emails cannot be recalled.

## Replace an expired or unsuitable link

Create a [new link](./create-link.md) with the right permissions and expiry, then
send it to the recipient. Revoke any old link that should no longer work.

::: tip Review other access too
A person may still have access through their account. Review [their client
access](../clients/manage-users.md) too. Files already downloaded remain on their device.
:::
